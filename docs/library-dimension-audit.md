# Library dimension audit — 6 September 2026

## Scope and confidence

This is a catalogue audit, not a claim that all 84 physical copies have been measured. The initial pass checked 31 records with an ISBN in the existing metadata, including three whose stored ISBN needed correction to find the physical edition. A follow-up added nine candidate editions. Both library views now share the resulting dimension resolver.

Dimensions below are **width × height × thickness**, in millimetres, except where a source is explicitly described otherwise. Original source order has been normalized. Retailer listings can contain trim sizes, cover-inclusive measurements, rounded values, or errors. Matching a catalogue ISBN is not proof of the ISBN of the user's copy: the shelf photos do not show barcodes.

## Applied locally

- 40 catalogue records are stored in `app/library/catalogue-dimensions.json`, each with source URLs, an edition-confidence status, and notes.
- 38 books now use catalogue-listed front dimensions; 33 also use catalogue-listed spine thicknesses. These include provisional, inferred edition matches, not just corroborated listings.
- The remaining 51 spine thicknesses are still photo estimates. Where a front height was corrected but thickness remains uncertain, the resolver recalibrates the estimate to preserve the observed photo thickness-to-height ratio. It does not average conflicting catalogue depths.
- The Silmarillion and The Graphic Design Bible retain their original estimates because the photographed editions are not securely identified. The other 44 books have no adopted catalogue record yet.
- Three physical-edition ISBNs were corrected in the source and generated book metadata. Existing cover-source URLs remain provenance for the previously downloaded images, not dimension evidence.
- Both `/library` and `/library/prototype` use `app/library/dimensions.ts`. The grid uses physical width and height; the prototype also uses depth for the 3D book and shelf spacing. Dimension text remains hidden, and the reduced rotation is unchanged. Nothing was pushed or deployed.

The table below preserves the initial audit: **Before W × H × T** means the values before these edits, not the live dimensions. The JSON is the authoritative record of the applied choices and includes the nine follow-up entries: Dissolving Classroom, Attack on Titan, My Hero Academia, Space Boy, Uzumaki, Get in Trouble, Interior Chinatown, Artemis, and The Immortal Life of Henrietta Lacks.

## What is safe to conclude

- The uniform 133 × 203 mm values assigned to many Stripe Press books are not reliable; retrieved listings usually put these editions near 158–162 × 234–244 mm.
- Several spine estimates are substantially low: The Dream Machine 33 → 39 mm, Working in Public 19 → 24–25 mm, The Big Score 27 → 37 mm, and Where Is My Flying Car? 25 → 31 mm.
- Some estimates were close: Hybrid 62 mm vs 63.5–64 mm, and Get Together 24 mm vs approximately 23–25 mm.
- Do not describe any value as an exact measurement of the user's copy. Retailer agreement is useful but can reflect a shared metadata feed, and printings may differ.
- For uncertain editions, a barcode/ISBN photo is the next reliable check. For conflicting thicknesses under the same ISBN, a ruler measurement of the closed book is better than another rounded shipping listing.

## Remaining work

44 books still have no adopted catalogue record, and two researched editions are on hold. The five front-only corrections also still need a reliable thickness. Candidate-edition matches and single-catalogue records remain provisional until the physical ISBN or measurements are confirmed. Preliminary title searches for User Experience Design, Digital Design, Maps, Annotated, and several other titles did not establish sufficiently reliable matching dimensions. No mass substitution from a similarly titled book or ebook record should be made.
