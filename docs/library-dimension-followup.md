# Remaining library dimensions — research follow-up

Checked 6 September 2026. Scope: the 51 displayed books missing catalogue front dimensions and/or thickness in the previous 38/33 audit. This is a research report, not an update to the website's measurements.

**Catalogue dimensions do not verify the user's physical copy.** ISBNs below are candidate print editions unless already recorded. Covers alone can span printings; some current cover files originate from ebooks. Values are width × height × thickness in millimetres (inches converted at 25.4 and rounded to 0.1). A dash means not established. Rounded library heights are not exact trim measurements. Retailer agreement can reflect one shared feed. Do not silently combine different bindings, editions, trim sizes, or shipping dimensions.

## Next step

The evidence above improves the catalogue coverage, but it does not justify claiming all remaining copies verified. Highest-priority ISBN/copyright-page checks: Eureka Seven, Larousse, The Silmarillion, The Left Hand of Darkness, Le Petit Prince, both Genki books, Introduction to Algorithms, The Chinese Exhibition, and The Graphic Design Bible. A closed-book ruler measurement is preferable for unresolved spine thicknesses, especially Practice and the conflicting Stripe Press titles.
